Read this ShabbatMay 16–17, 2026
Summary

On Mount Sinai, God teaches Moses the laws of the Sabbatical year: every seventh year, all work on the land shall cease, and its produce becomes free for the taking. After seven such cycles comes the Jubilee — a year when all servants are set free and ancestral lands return to their original owners.

Behar reminds us that the land — and time itself — ultimately belongs to God. We are guests and stewards, invited to rest, release, and trust.
