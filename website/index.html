Why Kesher

Technology should serve your soul, not steal it.

Kesher — the Hebrew word for connection — was built for people who want their devices to support their spiritual life instead of distracting from it.

We believe a small reminder, in the right moment, can be the difference between a missed mitzvah and a meaningful day. So we made the smallest, gentlest, most beautiful nudge we could imagine — and we wrapped it in a streak, a buddy, and a community.

One day at a time. One prayer at a time. One Shabbat at a time.
