Two paths, one app

Built for how you actually live your faith.

Kesher is not one-size-fits-all. Pick Judaism or Christianity at setup and the whole app adapts — language, reminders, content, and community.

✡︎

Jewish path

Shabbat Shalom — the full Kesher experience for Jewish living.

  • Shabbat candle-lighting and havdalah times for your location
  • Tefillin streaks and daily photo buddies
  • Modeh Ani, Shema, and prayer reminders
  • Weekly Torah portion and community videos
  • Shabbat Mode app blocking
  • Congregations, friends, and group chat
✝

Christian path

Walk with Christ — Scripture, quiet time, and church community.

  • Weekly rest and personal quiet-time focus blocks
  • Daily Scripture and devotional content
  • Prayer partners for accountability check-ins
  • Church groups and friend chat
  • Screen Time blocking for prayer and focus
  • Guided onboarding tailored to your path
Shared foundation

The tools that keep you consistent.

Whether you are preparing for Shabbat or guarding a Sabbath rest, Kesher gives you focus, friends, and gentle structure.

Focus & Rest Blocks

Block distracting apps for Shabbat, weekly rest, prayer time, or a custom quiet-time session. Your phone rests so you can too.

Community & Buddies

Join congregations or church groups, add friends, and stay accountable with tefillin buddies or prayer partners through daily check-ins and chat.

Streaks & Reminders

Gentle prompts for prayer, tefillin, and rest — timed for your day, not disruptive. Build streaks that reflect real consistency.

Chat & Notifications

Message friends and groups in-app. Visible notification badges help you see when someone checked in or sent a message.

Custom Quiet Time

Set a focus timer for prayer, Scripture, or personal quiet time — separate from your weekly rest block.

Scripture & Torah

Jewish users get the weekly parasha. Christian users get daily Scripture and devotionals — always at your fingertips.

Why Kesher

Technology should serve your soul, not steal it.

Kesher — the Hebrew word for connection — was built for people who want their devices to support their spiritual life instead of distracting from it.

We believe a small reminder, in the right moment, can be the difference between a missed habit and a meaningful day. So we made gentle nudges, streaks, buddies, and community — for Jewish and Christian users alike.

One day at a time. One prayer at a time. One rest day at a time.
